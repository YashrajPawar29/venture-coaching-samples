# Venture Coaching Samples

A small set of coaching materials I created to show how I would support early-stage student venture teams through the Lean Startup Build-Measure-Learn cycle, and how I use AI as a thinking tool without letting it replace customer evidence.

> **Note:** These are portfolio samples. The venture teams, interviews and pitches are **fictional**, written to show my coaching approach. No real people or student work are included.

## Tools I built for this work

- **[Customer Discovery Copilot](https://github.com/YashrajPawar29/customer-discovery-copilot)** labels interview statements as evidence, opinion or compliment, flags leading questions, and (optionally) uses Claude to map assumptions.
- **[Pitch Readiness Reviewer](https://github.com/YashrajPawar29/pitch-readiness-reviewer)** scores a pitch against an 8-criterion investor rubric and flags red-flag claims.

## My coaching principles

1. **Ask, don't answer.** The team owns the venture. My job is to ask the question that shows them their next step.
2. **Evidence over enthusiasm.** What a customer *did* beats what they *said they would do*. Compliments are worth nothing.
3. **AI is a hypothesis generator, not a source of evidence.** Use it to brainstorm, synthesize and prototype, then check every conclusion with real people.
4. **Fail fast, kindly.** An assumption that turned out wrong is learning, as long as the team acts on it.
